What this checks, and what it doesn't

It checks: SOL leaving your wallet, token approvals, ownership changes, and token transfers made directly by the transaction, for SPL Token and Token-2022.

It doesn't check: token moves made inside another program's instructions, token balance changes, Token-2022 extension behavior, or whether a website or program is trustworthy.

A passing result is not a guarantee. Use it as one check among several, and never sign anything you don't understand.

Second Look simulates transactions on Solana mainnet. It never signs or sends anything.
